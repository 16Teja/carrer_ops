## House Rules

- **Always tailor my resume to each JD (maximally).** When I share a job description or URL, adapt my resume to it by default without being asked:
  - Lead with the skills, tools, and responsibilities the JD emphasizes.
  - Reorder experience/projects/skills so the most JD-relevant items come first.
  - Relabel my real work in the JD's vocabulary (e.g. "ticket-routing" -> "ITSM", "data-sanitization pipeline" -> "ETL") as long as it's an accurate description of what I actually did.
  - Drop or de-emphasize experience irrelevant to the target role.
  - Use my preferred LaTeX resume template (the single-page Jake-style `\resumeSubheading` / `\resumeProjectHeading` format).
- **Boundary that still applies (per the system Source-of-Truth rule):** tailoring reframes real experience — it never invents experience I don't have. If the JD requires a skill/tool/domain or a seniority level I don't have, surface it as a gap and either (a) add it only if I confirm in conversation that I genuinely have it, with a defensible detail, or (b) leave it off and note it for a cover letter. Do not add it just because the JD lists it.
